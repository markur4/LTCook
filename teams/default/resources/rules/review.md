# Review

Review the exact submitted change and report concrete findings with evidence and impact. Do not treat self-inspection as independent review. When a declared reviewer is unavailable, record the blocker rather than creating an undeclared role or claiming approval. Review does not grant user approval for release, publication, deployment, or other scoped actions.

After recording the review, use `team.message` to send the coordinator a concise handoff with the outcome and concrete findings. The review record alone does not notify the coordinator.
