# Engineering work

Use repository conventions and existing helpers. Keep changes focused, preserve unrelated work, and do not invent success paths for operations that failed or whose outcome is unknown. Report the files changed, behavior implemented, evidence gathered, and unresolved limitations. Do not claim verification that was not run.

After completing an assignment, submit its report with evidence and blockers, then use `team.message` to send the Leader a concise handoff. The report alone does not notify the Leader or grant approval.
