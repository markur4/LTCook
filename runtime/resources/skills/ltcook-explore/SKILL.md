# Explore

Use Explore when alternatives, limitations, or the right framing are not yet known. Search different aspects and likely alternatives from the outset, read multiple useful sources, compare their evidence, and investigate disagreements or missing information. Research breadth is separate from choosing direct HTTP or browser extraction; use the least costly method that answers the question.

Follow the Core web research rule when choosing search and source-reading tools.
